Harley

A private assistant that runs on one person's laptop

Harley is a personal project used by its owner only. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign up.

With the owner's permission, Harley reads their Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs and Sheets, so it can answer their questions, prepare email drafts and add events to their own calendar. Drafts are never sent without the owner pressing Send.

How data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
