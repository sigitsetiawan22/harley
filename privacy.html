Harley

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Who uses Harley

Harley is a personal assistant run by one person on their own laptop. Only the owner's own Google accounts are connected. It is not offered to the public.

Google data Harley can access

  • Gmail: reading messages, searching, managing labels, and creating drafts. Harley does not send email on its own; the owner sends drafts themselves.
  • Google Calendar: reading events and adding events to the owner's own calendar, without inviting other people.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets: searching and reading files.
  • Google Contacts: reading contact names when the owner asks about someone.

How the data is used

Data is used only to answer the owner's requests, such as summarising an email, finding a file or checking the day's agenda. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

To answer a request, the relevant part of the data may be sent to a third-party AI model provider that processes the request and returns an answer. Only the part needed for that request is sent.

Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens and any notes Harley keeps are stored on the owner's laptop. Harley has no server of its own and no database of other people's data.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions. After that Harley can no longer read anything from that account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Harley.
